In stage 2, some small stimuli are activated, opening up some sodium channels, meaning a small increase in potential occurs. In stage 2, a stimulus causes some initial sodium channels to open, meaning a small increase in potential occurs. Once the overall potential reaches the threshold value of -55mV, the **voltage-gated** channels rapidly open maximally allowing a huge surge of potential, 'action potential' to occur in the neuron, causing a sharp increase in stage 3.
