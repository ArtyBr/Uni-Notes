The **weakest precondition** for a program $P$ to establish postcondition Post is a precondition **guaranteeing** that Post **holds** and that is **implied** by **any other** **possible precondition**

To work out weakest precondition:
- Write part of the post-condition
	- But the $X$ or variables in the condition that are changed in the assignment, and you replace them by the assignment
