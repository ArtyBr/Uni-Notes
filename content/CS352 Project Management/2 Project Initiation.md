# Knowledge areas for managing projects

![[Pasted image 20251014121837.png]]

The **P**roject **M**anagement **B**ody **O**f **K**nowledge (PMBOK) by the Project Management Insitute
What is it?
- A **framework**
- Split into 10 **Knowledge Areas** (KAs)
	- Each split into 5 **Process Groups** (PGs)
		- Identifies 49 individual **processes**
		- For each **process**:
			- **Inputs**
			- **Tools, Techniques, Best practices**
			- **Outputs** (document)

5 **Process Groups**:
1. **Intitation** - Business case - *Why* do the project?
2. **Planning** - Balance Time, Cost, Scope, Risk, ... (KAs)
3. **Execution** - Where the *real work* gets done
4. **Monitoring and Controlling** - Monitor and review *progress*
5. **Closing** - Delivery, audits and lessons learned

### Project Mandate

>[!note] Mandate
An official **order** or **commission** to **do something**

**Project** mandate:
- Statement of Work (SOW)
- What exactly **is** the project?
- What is **included** in this project and what is **excluded**? (The scope)
- **Constraints**? (Money, time)
- **Assumptions**
- Known **risks**/issues
### **Business case**
- Project must deliver **value**
	- Is the *effort, budget, time* **worth it?**
- Other competing projects?

*Start* of project: Sets out **ultimate goals** for stakeholders
*During* project: Guides key project **decisions**
*End* of project: **Learning linchpin** for business

These docs set out *why* we're doing this project

# Project Charter
The **Project Charter** formally *authorizes* the existence of the project and provides PM with the authority to apply resources

Outputs:
- Project **purpose** or **justification**
- Measurable **objectives** and **success criteria**
- High-level **requirements**, **boundaries**, **risks**, **constraints**
- Summary **milestone** schedule and **budget**
- **Stakeholder** list, **authority** of sponsor, assigned **project manager**, signing off **authority**

# Stakeholder Management

What project stakeholder might there be?
- **Sponsor** promotes, provides resources, leads through initiation
- **Customers and Users** for the project product, service or result
- **Sellers** provide components or services necessary for the project
- **Partners** provide expertise or service such as installation, training or support
- **Internal groups** that are effected by the activities of the project team such as marketing, manufacturing, sales, customer service
- **Functional managers** such as HR, finance, accounting or procurement that provide service to the project
- **Others** that have an interest in the outcome, such as regulators or the general public
