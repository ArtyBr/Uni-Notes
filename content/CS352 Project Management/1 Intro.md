# Examination/Assessment
- Group Presentation (10%)
	- 5 minute group presentation + Q&A
	- Team shares the **same grade**
	- Accessed by tutors
- Individual **Reflective Report** (20%)
	- Individual work (*based* on team's work)
	- Four 500-word chapter covering 
		- *Project Initiation*
		- *Project Planning*
		- *Project Execution*
		- *Project Monitoring and Control*
	- Can you **apply** relevant project management methods, **reflect** on your group project experience, and critically **evaluate** your approach and decisions using evidence?
	- In order to get better marks:
		- Try to do something *better* as a team,
		- Or something better *yourself* in your team
	- **Peer Assessed**
- Exam (70%)
