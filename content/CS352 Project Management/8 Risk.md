*'Risk is the effect of uncertainty on objectives*

Uncertainty - something that is *not definite* (has a **probability** of occurrence)
**Threats** vs **Opportunities** - Risk is not necessarily negative, can have unexpected benefits
**Known** and **unknown** risks - things that are *identified* and *under control* vs those that aren't.
**Issue** - If the event that caused risk **occurs**, that becomes an **issue**

**RACI** Matrix
- Clarifies roles and responsibilities for each item in the WBS
- Records who is **Responsible**, **Accountable**, **Consulted**, and **Informed**
- All tasks should have **at least one** person **responsible** (person who actually completes the task)
- Every task should have one and **only one** person **accountable** (the individual who is ultimately answerable for the task)

The **Pareto Principle** states that there are certain inequalities that apply with '20%' of something applying to '80%' of the situations.

'It takes 80% of the time to complete the last 20% of the project'

The conclusion is to **prioritise**

**Failure Mode Effects Analysis** (FMEA) is an inductive reasoning (forward logic) single point of failure analysis

Assign a score from 1-10 to:
Remember, **big number** is **worse**
- **Severity** S
- **Occurrence Probability** O
- **Detection rate** (hard to detect rate) D


Calculate:
- **Criticality** $C=S\times O$
- **Risk Priority Number** $RPN=C\times D$

Highest RPN - Failure is not detectable by inspection - very severe - and the occurrence is almost sure

![[Pasted image 20251124160043.png]]

**Extended** FMEA extends FMEA by:
- Adding **corrective actions**
- Deciding the **feasibility** (really the **in**feasibility)  of the action (how hard it would be to achieve)
- Choose the corrective action which gives the **most feasible reduction in risk**
