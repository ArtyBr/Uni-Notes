# Learning objectives

- **Image fundamentals**: Understanding how a digital image is formed and stored
- **Image Enhancement**: Understanding how to remove artefacts from images and enhance them for forensic applications
- **Forgery Detection**: To be able to answer if an image is authentic, to localise which part of the image has been tampered
- **Source Identification & Deepfake Detection**: To be able to identify the imaging source (device); to be able to distinguish real images from deepfakes

**Digital Forensics**
The use of scientific methods to collect probative facts from **digital evidence** often in relation to cyber, physical and cyber-physical crime

Digital forensics is used to detect evidence of a crime, attribute evidence to suspects, confirm statements, determine intent, identify source devices or authenticate data.
